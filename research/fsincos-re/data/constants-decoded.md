## Reading the deviations

A coefficient exactly equal to its Taylor value shows 0 ulps. Remez/minimax
fits show small but *systematic* deviations, largest at the tail (highest
degree), because the fit redistributes the truncation error across the
interval. The hi/lo split of PP_1 and the 2-ulp-shy C_1 are *engineering*
choices (exactness of products), not fit artifacts.
