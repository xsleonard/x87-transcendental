## H1479--H1481 topology/isomorphism follow-up

H1479 asks whether the leading XOR is merely another spelling of a natural
single signal in an arithmetic-isomorphic P5 tree.  The answer is negative for
the bounded representation family tested here.

The two XOR operands are not inputs to one compressor cell.  The
`R.l1_4.sum[cut]` operand is produced from PP16--PP19 and follows
`l1_4 -> l2_2 -> l4_0`.  The `R.l2_1.carry[cut-3]` operand is produced from
PP8--PP15 and follows `l2_1 -> l3_0 -> l4_0`.  Their first common consumer is
the final `l4_0` compressor, but they reach it at columns separated by three.
Consequently the public four-level tree does not directly compute their XOR.

The analogous XOR was replayed under four exact negative-correction routes
and all sixteen level-one/level-two distinguished-D assignments.  Only D-slot
assignment `(0,0)`, the patent transcription used by the emulator, fits all
eighteen labels; the four correction routes are indistinguishable at the high
observed columns.  Thus the fit is representation-specific, although that
specificity is not itself proof of the silicon netlist.

H1479 also searched every node bit and final CPA sum/carry/propagate/generate/
kill/carry-in bit in seventy arithmetic-exact P5 tree layouts: 866 signals per
layout, 121,240 literals including complements.  Eleven alternate single-wire
signals fit the original eighteen labels.  Every one has explicit functional
counterexamples in H1476's 22 disjoint exact-lattice rows: nine fail 13/22 and
two fail 14/22.  No single signal equals R1475 over the combined forty rows.
This disproves those proposed isomorphisms without using new hardware labels.
It does not disprove separate control logic that deliberately XORs the two
remote wires; R1475 remains a possible but label-selected control hypothesis.

H1480 evaluated the eleven alternate aliases on the already-frozen ten H1477
tuples before any label was opened.  They form five prediction classes, all
distinct from R1475's `1100111001` merge vector.  The H1477 manifest and input
files were not changed.  H1481 is a supplemental prewritten scorer for these
classes; it remains unexecuted because H1477 remains `FROZEN_UNOPENED`.
